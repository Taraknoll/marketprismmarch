1 · Why Market Prism exists

Built for Decisions Before It Was Built for Data Buyers

Market Prism began as an operating system for understanding changing market information — not as a retrospective dataset created for an institutional backtest.

Founder lens
More than a decade advising companies across industries on strategy, positioning, digital behavior and information systems.
Original purpose
Reduce the information disadvantage faced by retail investors by turning fragmented evidence into understandable, actionable market states.
Real-world incentive
Used in my own research and trading, so the objective is prospective usefulness, data integrity and operational reliability.

The operating system came first. Institutional licensing came afterward.

Not designed around any evaluator's specification. The architecture, constructs and standards are the founder's own.
2 · What the system measures

Information Has a Measurable Lifecycle

Market Prism measures how narratives form, strengthen, persist, decay and break — and converts those states into quantitative features.

Not sentiment scoring an LLM stock picker Is a measurement layer for the information state surrounding each security
