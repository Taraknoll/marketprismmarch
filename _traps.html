How a story gets flagged.

Every trap on this page was flagged by the same deterministic pipeline that powers the dashboard — no analyst picks, no editorial judgment, same inputs in, same flags out.

Anchored to the filings

Each claim is scored against the company's actual SEC filings and a model fair value. A story trading far above what the filings support raises the fair-value gap.

Coordination detection

When multiple outlets publish suspiciously similar framing inside a tight window, the coverage is flagged as suspicious or coordinated rather than organic.

Decay & exhaustion modeling

Narratives lose force on a measurable curve. The pipeline estimates how many days of fuel a story has left before it exhausts.
